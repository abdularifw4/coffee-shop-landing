Our Story

From Indonesian
Highlands to Your Cup

Brewed Awakening started in 2023 from a tiny corner in Jakarta with one belief: everyone deserves truly exceptional coffee.

We source directly from farmers in Gayo, Toraja, and Flores — paying fair prices and building long-term relationships. Every batch is roasted in-house to unlock each bean's unique flavor.
