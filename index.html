Our Story

Crafted with
Passion & Purpose

Started in 2023 from a tiny corner in Jakarta, Brewed Awakening was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves access to truly exceptional coffee.

We source our beans directly from farmers in Gayo, Toraja, and Flores — paying fair prices and building long-term relationships. Every batch is roasted in-house to unlock each bean's unique flavor profile.
